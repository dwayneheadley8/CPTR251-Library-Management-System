## 🚀 Core Features

### 📚 **Book Management**
- Add, edit, and remove books from the collection
- Track book availability and location
- Manage multiple copies of the same title
- Category and genre classification
- ISBN and barcode support

### 👥 **Patron Management** 
- User registration and profile management
- Membership tracking and renewals
- Borrowing history and preferences
- Contact information management
- Patron classification (student, faculty, public)

### 🔄 **Circulation System**
- Check-out and check-in processes
- Due date management and renewals
- Hold and reservation system
- Overdue notifications
- Fine calculation and payment tracking

### 🔍 **Search & Discovery**
- Advanced search by title, author, ISBN, genre
- Filter by availability and location
- Recommendation system
- Popular and trending books
- Recently added items

### 📊 **Reporting & Analytics**
- Circulation statistics
- Popular books and authors
- Patron activity reports
- Overdue items tracking
- Financial summaries
